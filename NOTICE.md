MIDI++ Luau is an unofficial Luau adaptation of MIDIPlusPlus.

Original project: https://github.com/Zephkek/MIDIPlusPlus
Source snapshot: https://github.com/Zephkek/MIDIPlusPlus/tree/512c4c7dd4164860c5a178c4963e3b0403d77319

The 61/88-key piano layouts and five 32-step velocity tables are adapted from
the original project's ConfigHandler.cpp and PlaybackCore.cpp. Credit belongs
to Zephkek and the original contributors. The MIDI parser, playback state
machine, executor adapter, Roblox UI, documentation, and demo were newly
written for this adaptation. This is not an official MIDIPlusPlus release.
